## Good vs bad acceptance criteria

| Fix | Bad (can't test) | Good (pass or fail) |
|---|---|---|
| Missed-call text-back | "Missed calls get handled." "Customers stop complaining." | "A missed call during business hours gets a text back within 2 minutes, 5 test calls in a row." "Each missed call shows up in [CRM or sheet] with number and time." |
| Lead follow-up sequence | "Follow-ups work well." "More leads turn into jobs." | "A test lead that doesn't reply gets follow-up texts on day 1, 3 and 7 (checked with shortened timing for the test)." "Follow-ups stop within 5 minutes of the lead replying." |
| Booking or reminder flow | "Booking is easy." "Fewer no-shows." | "A test booking made through the link shows up on [calendar] within 1 minute." "The customer gets a reminder text 24 hours and 2 hours before, on 3 test bookings." |

Bad criteria are opinions ("works well") or results you don't control (more jobs, fewer no-shows). Good criteria are things you can see happen on a test.

## When a client says "it's not working"

- Stay calm and don't guess. Ask: "Which check isn't passing? Walk me through what you did, when, and what you saw."
- If a listed check is failing: retest it together first. It only counts if it fails again. If it does, it's real. Fix it fast, retest that check with them, and send a new "ready for sign-off" notice with your new test results.
- If the fix is causing harm (texts going to the wrong people, duplicate messages to customers): fix it right away, even if it isn't a listed check. Billing keeps running unless it also breaks a listed check.
- If it's not a listed check (a new idea, another tool, "can it also do this"): "Good idea. That isn't one of the checks we agreed on, so I'll add it to the change list with its own date. The original fix is passing." Put it on the change list and reply in writing.
- If an outside vendor is down: log it as a waiting notice, help them report it, and tell them in writing.
- After acceptance: if an accepted check stops passing because of your work, start on it within 1 business day of them telling you, under the plan. Billing keeps running. A goodwill credit if something is down for more than 5 business days is Abhi's call, not a requirement.
- Keep all of it in writing (email or text), with dates.

## Texting rules

- A text-back to someone who just called is lower risk, because they reached out first and it's a reply.
- Follow-up sequences (day 1, 3, 7) and anything promotional are higher risk.
- Federal robotext rules (TCPA) can require written consent for marketing texts.
- Texas expanded its telemarketing law to cover texts in 2025.
- Carriers require business texting registration (A2P 10DLC) or messages get blocked.
- Keep texts transactional, not salesy. Send nothing before 9am or after 9pm.
- Get an attorney to confirm before the first client with a follow-up sequence.
