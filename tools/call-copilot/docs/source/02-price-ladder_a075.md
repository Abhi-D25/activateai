## When to pick which price
Use right after the checkup, once the leak and its monthly cost are agreed in the owner's own numbers. These steps only restate the tier rules on this page. If they ever seem to disagree, the rules in Sections 1 and 2 win.
1. **Is the agreed leak under $1,000 a month?** Don't sell a monthly plan. Give one free tip and the first three things to set up themselves, and check back in 60 days.
2. **One leak, out-of-the-box tools** (one trigger and up to 2 tools the owner already has, or a standard voice setup on the platform's ready-made connectors): **Get Covered $199.**
3. **One leak that needs a custom build or integration, 3 or more tools, or a multi-step flow** (also more than about 300 leads or messages a month, or 2 or more locations): **Get Covered Plus $349.** For voice, the calendar, existing connectors, after-hours rules and texting don't count as extra tools or rules. Only a custom-built integration moves a voice setup up.
4. **Two or more leaks that add up to $3,750 a month or more:** **Full Fix $749** (up to 3 leaks, builds start Nov 10 or later). If two leaks add up to less, fix the bigger one on Get Covered or Plus and offer Add a leak later.
5. **Check the 1/5 rule.** The monthly price divided by the agreed leak must be 1/5 or less, aiming for about 1/10. So $199 needs a leak of at least $1,000, $349 needs $1,750, and $749 needs $3,750. If it fails, shrink the scope to the most expensive piece. Never shrink the price.
6. **Founding price:** first 5 clients only, until Jan 31, 2027 at the latest. Plus $299, Full Fix $599. Get Covered stays $199. Held 6 months after acceptance, then standard with 30 days' written notice, in return for a short testimonial with a real number. Run the 1/5 check at the standard price too, because the price steps up.
Before giving a date, check the caps (3 builds at once, a Full Fix counts as 2; 2 new starts a month) and the India rules (Oct 7 to 25 Get Covered only; no target dates Oct 26 to Nov 2).

## 2. Why this ladder
### What makes a leak $199 versus $349
Abhi picks the tier after the checkup, using three questions. Any "yes" makes it Plus.
1. Does the fix touch 3 or more tools (for example web form, CRM, texting app, calendar)?
2. Does it run over time or by rules (a day 1, 3, 7 follow-up, reminders before bookings, different handling after hours)?
3. Is the volume high (more than about 300 leads or messages a month) or are there 2 or more locations?
**Voice exception:** a voice receptionist is judged differently. Taking a booking request (the caller's chosen time) or a callback, CRM or job tool connectors that already exist on the platform, after-hours rules and texting the owner are out-of-the-box settings, not extra tools or rules, so a standard voice setup stays Get Covered $199. It moves to Plus, or into Full Fix, only when it needs an integration that isn't available out of the box and has to be custom built. Checking open times depends on the calendar connection and is confirmed per client.
Examples:
- Get Covered ($199): missed call during business hours gets a text back and a row in the owner's Google Sheet. Web form lead gets an instant text and an alert to the owner. Unanswered calls go to a voice receptionist that takes the booking request (the caller's chosen time) or a callback and texts the owner to confirm, logs the caller through an existing CRM connector and follows after-hours rules.
- Get Covered Plus ($349): web and phone leads go into the CRM, get a follow-up on day 1, 3 and 7 that stops when they reply, and the owner gets a daily list of who is still open. A voice receptionist that has to push calls into a job tool with no ready-made connector, so the link is custom built.
Volume is a tier factor only because it adds monitoring time. Tool, texting and voice platform usage costs stay on the client's own card, per the approved scope template.
### Full Fix price and capacity
- **Price: $749/mo.** Three Plus-level leaks bought one at a time would be $1,047/mo, so Full Fix reads as the better deal for anyone with three real leaks. To be honest about two leaks: two Plus leaks bought separately cost $698 a month against $749 for Full Fix, and the extra $51 buys the 2 change items a month, the monthly check-in and a third leak slot. It sits below the automation agencies found ($500 to $2,500/mo plus $1,500 to $5,000 setup) and far below fractional ops help ($4,000/mo found), and near Podium Pro's reported $599/mo, which is software only on an annual contract.
- **Monthly capacity, defined:**
	- Up to 3 problems in Section 2 of the scope, each with its own checks and target date.
	- After every leak in the scope is accepted: **2 change items a month.** A change item is one change-list request that uses tools already set up and takes about 2 hours or less (add a second web form to the lead flow, a new reminder text, a new column in the lead sheet). Bigger requests count as 2 items or get their own flat quote. Unused items do not roll over.
	- **Replies within 1 business day.** Change items done within 10 business days. A check that already passed and breaks because of our work: started within 1 business day (already in the scope template).
	- One 20-minute check-in a month, with the numbers.
- **Get Covered capacity**, for contrast: keep the one fix working, plus small tweaks (wording, hours, who gets the alert) up to 30 minutes a month (60 for Plus). Anything bigger goes on the change list and becomes "add a leak" or a move to Full Fix.
### Setup fee: none, and why
Recommended: **no setup fee.**
- The strongest line Abhi has is "you don't pay until it's fixed." A setup fee breaks that line.
- The owner's alternatives mostly say "no setup fee" out loud: Ruby, [Smith.ai](http://Smith.ai), Time etc and the managed offshore VA firm found all advertise it. The spend-less owner compares against those, not against agencies.
- Cash flow risk is handled without a fee: card on file at "confirmed," first month charged on acceptance day, and the existing 5-business-day deemed acceptance in the scope template.
- Options considered and not recommended now:
	- A refundable start deposit (one AutoLogix-style agency uses $500, refundable if they miss the agreed success criteria). Adds friction at the yes, and the refund rules need Harvey.
	- Hormozi's waived-fee offer (big setup fee, waived if the client commits to a term). It conflicts with the approved month-to-month terms. Worth a second look for Full Fix only after there is proof.
- Prepay "6 for 5" gives cash up front to owners who like a deal, without making anyone pay before the fix works.
### Founding price and the planned raise
- Founding prices (Plus $299, Full Fix $599) go to the first 5 clients only. Get Covered stays at $199 because it is already the floor.
- They are held for 6 months after acceptance, then step up to standard with 30 days' written notice. No lifetime price locks.
- New clients move to standard after 5 founding clients or Jan 31, 2027, whichever comes first.
- Raise standard prices for new clients when either is true: more than half of checkups close over the last 10, or Abhi hits the 10-client cap. Test any raise on new clients first. Review prices at least once a year.

### How a client moves up
1. **Free checkup.** Leak named, cost agreed.
2. **Get Covered** ($199 or $349). Billing starts at acceptance.
3. **30-day results check** (15 minutes, after acceptance). Show the number: calls caught, leads answered, hours saved. Ask for the testimonial if founding. Ask: "What's the next thing that's leaking?"
4. **Add a leak** (+$199 or +$349), or **move to Full Fix** once there are 2 or more leaks. On an upgrade, the client keeps paying the current rate until the new leak is accepted, then the Full Fix price starts. The new leak is the guaranteed problem.
5. **Full Fix** with monthly change items is where clients stay. Later, once there are 6 or more clients, a short, capped offer to existing clients once a quarter (for example "3 spots this month for an after-hours fix").
