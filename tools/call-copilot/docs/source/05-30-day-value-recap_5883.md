## Where to pull each number

| Number | Where to pull it | How |
|---|---|---|
| Missed calls texted back | Texting tool log, checked against call logs | Count text-backs sent in the 30-day window. Spot-check a few against missed calls in the call log. |
| Leads answered in under 5 minutes | CRM | For each new lead, compare the time it came in with the time of the first reply. Count the ones under 5 minutes. Note the total too (for example 41 of 44). |
| Follow-ups sent | Texting tool or CRM follow-up report | Count follow-up messages sent in the window. Leave out first replies so nothing is counted twice. |
| Bookings made | Booking calendar (and CRM source field) | Count bookings that came from a lead the fix touched (text-back, fast reply, follow-up, booking link). If you can't trace the source, ask the owner: "Of these, about how many turned into work?" and use their number. |
| Hours of busywork removed | Texting tool, CRM, or calendar task count | Number of tasks the fix did x minutes each, using the minutes the owner gave on the call. Example: 120 reminder texts x 2 minutes = 4 hours. Round down. |
| Typical job or order value | Checkup call notes (required note on every call) | Use the owner's number as they said it. Never bump it up. |
| Agreed monthly leak cost | Checkup call notes (Step 2, Name the leak) | The $/month you and the owner landed on. If they wouldn't ballpark, drop that sentence from the comparison line. |
| Plan price and acceptance date | Scope page, Sections 1 and 4 | Copy as written. |

## Rules for the numbers

- Every number comes from a log or from the owner. Never estimate a count. If you can't pull it, drop the line.
- Rough value = only the count that plausibly turned into work (bookings, or jobs the owner confirms) x the owner's job value. Never all leads x job value.
- Say "about" and "could be worth." Round down.
- If a count is 0, drop that line.
- If the owner never gave a job value, skip the rough value and comparison lines and send the counts only.
- If the rough value comes in under the plan price, say so plainly and offer a quick look at how to tune the fix. Don't stretch the numbers.
- Only share outside what the owner OK'd: anonymous, a testimonial, or nothing.

## Good vs bad recap lines

| Bad | Why it's bad | Good |
|---|---|---|
| "The system is working great." | An opinion. No numbers the owner can check. | "23 missed calls got a text back. 6 booked. Your average job is $350." |
| "The fix made you $8,050." (23 calls x $350) | Made-up revenue. Assumes every call became a paid job. | "6 of them booked. At your $350 average job, that could be worth about $2,100." |
| "Your revenue is up 40%." | We don't see their books. Invented. | "41 of 44 new leads got an answer in under 5 minutes." |
| "Our smart automation is crushing it." | Hype and buzzwords. Says nothing. | "112 reminder texts went out on their own. That's about 4 hours you didn't spend on it." |
